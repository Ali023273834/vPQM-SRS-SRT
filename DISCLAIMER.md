# Disclaimer

The vPQM calculator is provided for **plan quality assurance, education and research only**.

- **Not a medical device.** It has not been cleared or approved by any regulatory authority and must not be used as the basis for clinical decisions.
- **It does not accept, approve or reject treatment plans.** Plan acceptance remains the responsibility of qualified medical physicists and radiation oncologists, under local protocols and with all relevant clinical information.
- **It scores only the values entered.** It does not assess cumulative dose after re-irradiation, OARs that are not entered, target delineation, dose-calculation accuracy, deliverability or patient-specific clinical factors.
- **The benchmarks come from a single institution** and a single planning system and technique. They may not represent what is achievable elsewhere, and are undergoing multi-institutional validation.
- **The zone boundaries (75 and 85)** were set on a single-institution development cohort and are being tested prospectively.
- **No data are collected.** The tool runs entirely in the user's browser, and no data are transmitted or stored.
